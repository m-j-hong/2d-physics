This piece is a quiet meditation on the self; not as something fixed, but as something layered, responsive, and alive. Each ring is a layer of identity: habits, stories, defenses, memories, nested around a center that can’t quite be seen, only felt. The colors shift slowly because nothing essential about us ever changes all at once. Attention matters here. As you move closer to the center, the system becomes more saturated, more vivid, more awake; as you drift away, it dulls and flattens. Nothing is forced. Nothing breaks. It’s just a reminder that aliveness isn’t something you add to yourself—it’s something that emerges when you pay attention to what’s already there.
